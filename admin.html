    Profiles and bets are kept in the shared Google Sheet. Export a backup now and then. Import replaces everything in the sheet with the backup, so use it with care.

    SQUARES Squares Boards

    Make a 10 x 10 board for a game. Buddies claim squares on the Squares page; you draw the numbers when it is full (that locks it), and winners are called from the score at each interval you pick.

    Intervals to pay out, and the share of the pot each one wins (shares must add up to 100%).

    POOL NFL Pool

    Hide the Pool from the menu and block the page for everyone. Picks are kept and come back when you show it again.

    ODDS Sportsbook Odds

    Odds are pulled automatically once on each game day, 3 hours before the first kickoff. Credits are limited, so only pull by hand if that missed.
